## Description

Stray reveals the world as you walk through it.

Dark fog covers every place you haven't been. Each step uncovers a little more. Over time, your map becomes a quiet record of everywhere you've explored — your neighborhood, your city, the places you've traveled.

When you're ready to discover something new, start a Stray session. A compass points you toward unexplored territory. Follow it or ignore it. There's no wrong way to wander.

Features:
- Fog-of-war map that reveals as you walk
- Works in the background — never miss a step
- Frequency-weighted compass that guides you to new places
- Photo integration — see your geotagged photos on the map
- Heat map visualization of your most-visited areas
- Daily timeline to replay your exploration history
- Per-city stats and session tracking
- iCloud sync across your devices
- No accounts, no ads, no tracking

Stray is anti-optimization. No leaderboards. No streaks to protect. No pressure. Just you and the map.

Inspired by the Situationist concept of the derive — an unplanned journey through a landscape, guided by curiosity and the pull of the unknown.

## App Review Notes

Stray uses "Always" location permission to reveal a fog-of-war map while the user walks, including when the app is in the background. This is the core function of the app — without background location, cells are only revealed while the app is in the foreground, which defeats the purpose.

The app also works with "When In Use" location (with reduced functionality), and functions without location permission at all (the map remains fully fogged).

No account is required. There is no server or backend. All data is stored on-device and synced via iCloud.

To test: walk with the app open and observe fog clearing around your location. Lock the phone, walk for a few minutes, reopen — cells revealed in the background should appear.
