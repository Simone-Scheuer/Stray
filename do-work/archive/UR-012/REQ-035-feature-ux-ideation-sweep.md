## Implementation Summary — Curated Feature Ideas

### Tier 1: High-Impact, Photo-Momentum Features

These build directly on what's working (photos + place-based media).

**1. Photo Timeline Sync**
When scrubbing the daily timeline, show that day's photos as a horizontal strip below the map. Tapping a photo centers the map on that cell. Photos become the emotional anchor of timeline replay — not just "where you were" but "what you saw."

**2. Photo Journal / Cell Notes**
Tap a cell → add a short text note alongside its photos. Not a full journal app — just a sentence or two: "Best coffee I've ever had" or "This alley had incredible graffiti." Notes appear in cell inspector below photos. Makes cells feel like *places*, not coordinates.

**3. Photo Map Markers**
When photo mode is on, show small circular thumbnails pinned to the map at photo locations (like Google Maps photo dots, but your own). Tapping opens the photo directly. This turns the map into a visual scrapbook — much more alive than the purple density heatmap alone.

**4. Shareable Exploration Cards**
Generate a beautiful card image from a session, day, or city: your fog map snapshot + stats + best photos, formatted for Instagram/stories. "I explored 47 new cells in Portland today" with a map crop. This is Stray's viral loop — photo mode proved visual output resonates.

---

### Tier 2: Deepen the Core Loop

These make the existing mechanics richer.

**5. Session Path Trace**
During an active Stray session, draw a thin polyline showing your actual walking path on the map (using the location stream you already have). After the session, the path persists in session history. This makes sessions feel like *journeys* with a visible trail, not just a cell count delta.

**6. Compass Target Modes**
Add a small menu on the compass: "Nearest undiscovered" (current), "Random direction" (truly psychogeographic — pick a random bearing), "Revisit" (point toward your least-visited revealed cell). Different modes for different moods. "Random direction" is the most Situationist option.

**7. Session Pause/Resume**
Add a pause button to suspend a Stray session without ending it. Resume later. Simple but missing — coffee stop shouldn't end your exploration.

**8. "Discovered Today" Glow**
Cells revealed in the current day get a subtle pulsing edge or brighter tint, distinct from older cells. Makes your daily progress visible at a glance without opening stats. Fades after 24h.

---

### Tier 3: Meaning & Reflection

These turn data into personal insight — the "life cartography" promise.

**9. Weekly/Monthly Recap**
Auto-generate a summary card (in-app, not push notification): "This week you explored 12 new cells across 3 neighborhoods. You took 8 photos. Your most-visited cell is near SE Hawthorne." Accessible from Stats. A gentle reflection prompt, not a productivity guilt trip.

**10. City Comparison View**
In Stats, tap a city to see its own mini-map with fog/heat, total cells, first visit date, and photos. For someone who travels (like you), this becomes a collection: "Cities I've mapped." Each city is a chapter in your life cartography.

**11. Time-of-Day Patterns**
A simple radial chart in Stats: when do you explore? Morning person or night walker? Based on RevealedCell timestamps. Not prescriptive — just a mirror. "You explore most between 2-5 PM on weekends."

**12. Milestone Markers on Timeline**
Auto-detect and mark significant days: "First cell ever," "100th cell," "New city discovered," "Longest session." Small badge icons on the timeline pills. Adds narrative structure to the timeline without gamification.

---

### Tier 4: Polish & Feel

Small things that make the app feel more alive.

**13. Compass Haptics**
When the compass bearing changes significantly (>45°), fire a subtle haptic tap. When you reach a beacon, fire a strong haptic. Makes the compass feel *physical* even when the phone is in your pocket.

**14. Session Ambient Sound Cue**
When starting a Stray session, play a brief ambient sound (wind, birds, footsteps) — 1-2 seconds. When ending, a different closing sound. Creates a ritual around the session start/end. Optional, off by default.

**15. Fog Edge Softening**
Currently fog has hard pixel edges at cell boundaries. Add a 2-3px Gaussian blur or gradient feather to the fog boundary where revealed cells meet fog. Makes the map feel more organic and less grid-like. (Previously attempted with rounded rects which caused gaps — this is a different approach using edge blending.)

**16. Cell Inspector Swipe Navigation**
When cell inspector is open, swipe left/right to move to adjacent cells without closing the sheet. Lets you "browse" a neighborhood's cells fluidly.

**17. Animated Fog Reveal**
When a new cell is revealed in real-time (not boot/background), briefly animate the fog clearing with a 200ms fade-out instead of instant appearance. Subtle but satisfying. ### Ideas Intentionally Left Out

These came up during exploration but conflict with Stray's philosophy or are premature:

- **Leaderboards / social comparison** — anti-optimization by design
- **Streak-loss warnings / daily reminders** — no guilt patterns
- **Turn-by-turn navigation** — compass points, doesn't navigate
- **Apple Watch companion** — cool but massive scope, premature
- **Offline maps** — requires tile caching infrastructure, not MVP-adjacent
- **AI-generated walking routes** — antithetical to "get lost on purpose"
- **Third-party integrations** (Strava, Apple Health) — zero-dependency philosophy
